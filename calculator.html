What each figure means

Gross booking value (GBV)
Total billed to customers. It can include taxes and money passed on to suppliers, so it is not revenue.
Recognized revenue
What the business earns. Principal basis: GBV minus taxes. Agent (net) basis: only the margin, because supplier money is collected on behalf of others.
Commission or acquisition cost
Percentage of GBV paid to channels or partners to win the booking.
Gross margin
Recognized revenue minus the direct cost the business carries.
Contribution margin
Gross margin minus commission / acquisition cost.
Net profit
Contribution margin minus fixed overhead (before income tax).

Formulas for the selected type

GBV = Bookings x Average booking value Pass-through = GBV x Pass-through % Recognized revenue = GBV - Pass-through (principal) | Gross margin amount (agent basis) Contribution = Gross margin - GBV x Commission % Net profit = Contribution - Fixed overhead

Assumptions and limitations

  • One month, steady state; no seasonality, timing of cash or refunds.
  • Changes are applied independently and instantly; demand, price and cost often move together in reality.
  • Commission is treated as a percentage of GBV; fixed overhead does not change between scenarios.
  • Income tax, financing costs and currency movement are excluded.
  • Conversion and utilization are capped at 100%.
  • Software does not create demand by itself; any uplift depends on how your team acts on the information.
