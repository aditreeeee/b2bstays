Different businesses, different value

The same platform matters in different ways depending on whether you run a property, sell packages, move guests or supply services.

Who benefits

Hotels and accommodation providers

  • Property, room information and CSV import to keep inventory records organised.
  • City Ledger and group-booking collection status for balances owed.
  • Packages, transportation and vendor coordination in one place.

Travel agents and tour operators

  • Custom Package Quote with quotation requests awaiting reply on the dashboard.
  • Package commission records for margin visibility.
  • Customer management and notifications for post-booking contact.

Package operators

  • Manage Packages, Package Bookings and Destination Activities.
  • Package Day Notifications to keep travellers informed on the day.
  • Package Commission to see what partners earn.
  • Quotation and booking data from which conversion and margin can be calculated.

Transportation businesses

  • Availability Calendar, Manual Blocks and Vehicle Allocation.
  • Vehicles, drivers, suppliers, locations, operating cities and rates as masters.
  • New Cab Booking and All Bookings.
  • Transportation reports to review activity; utilization is measurable from available vs booked days.

Vendors and suppliers

  • A Vendor Management module that organises supplier relationships for the operator.
  • Clearer requests and assignments can mean fewer misunderstandings.
  • For the operator: supplier response time and cost per booking can be tracked once recorded.

Corporate travel businesses Where relevant

  • City Ledger and accounts support billing to company accounts.
  • Group booking revenue and collection status help follow balances.
  • Document Library and notifications for itineraries and paperwork.
  • Corporate-specific features should be confirmed in a demo; none are claimed here.
Before and after

Unstructured vs structured operations

What can change when work moves from scattered tools into a managed workflow. The "After" columns describe the intended workflow and are limited to modules named in the portal. Measurable outcomes still depend on your usage.

Package quotations

Before
  1. Enquiry
  2. Manual quotation
  3. Untracked follow-up
  4. Unknown outcome
After
  1. Enquiry
  2. Structured quotation
  3. Follow-up tracking

Shown in portal Custom Package Quote; quotation requests awaiting reply. To confirm Recording of a confirmed or lost outcome per quote.

Financial control

After
